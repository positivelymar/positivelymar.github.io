Costa Rica
A protected realm

Almost 30% of Costa Rica's territory is protected. 28 National Parks span its 11 conservation areas, each with unique ecosystems and extraordinary biodiversity.

Small, yet striking...

When you think of traveling to Costa Rica, destinations like Manuel Antonio or La Fortuna might come to mind — spectacular, yet only a small part of what the country has to offer. With a territory of just 52,000 km², you'd think everywhere would be alike. You couldn't be more mistaken.

A multiverse within

Costa Rica has 12 different life zones (Holdridge classification). In a matter of hours you could move from Subalpine Rain Páramo to Tropical Wet Rainforest. These life zones coexist within each of its 11 conservation areas, hosting almost 30 national parks in total.

Traveling, the "Pura Vida" way

Plan your trip around the conservation areas and you'd experience: volcanic highlands, an active Arenal volcano, the biodiverse canals of Tortuguero, Caribbean mangroves, and Pacific whale watching — all in one trip.

Conservation Area 01

Guanacaste

Tropical Dry Forest & Seasonal Savannas

Defined by tropical dry forests, golden savannas, and drought-adapted wildlife. A striking contrast between arid and lush landscapes across seasons.

Conservation Area 02

Arenal Tempisque

Volcanic Highlands & Misty Cloud Forests

Volcanic highlands blended with cloud forest ecosystems shaped by Arenal Volcano's activity. Frequent mist, fertile soils, and rich biodiversity from jungle to thermal valleys.

Conservation Area 03

Central

Great Metropolitan Valley & Cloud Forest Reserves

Urban-adjacent highlands with protected cloud forest pockets. Despite human proximity, elevation and humidity support moss-covered trees, orchids, and surprising birdlife.

Conservation Area 04

La Amistad Pacífico

Cloud Forests & Mountain Biodiversity Hotspot

Steep mountains and dense cloud forests on the Pacific slope. Constant moisture from ocean winds feeds exceptional biodiversity — one of the least disturbed areas in the country.

Conservation Area 05

La Amistad Caribe

Caribbean Slopes & Ultra-Humid Cloud Forests

Wetter and more humid than its Pacific counterpart, with thick rainforests descending toward lowland valleys. Rare amphibians and dense jungle canopies shaped by constant rainfall.

Conservation Area 06

Osa

Lowland Rainforest & Untouched Biodiversity

One of the most biodiverse places on Earth. Remote lowland rainforest meets rugged coastline, preserving primary forest habitats with jaguars, scarlet macaws, and dense jungle life.

Conservation Area 07

Pacífico Central

Tropical Rainforest & Coastal Marine Ecosystems

Tropical rainforest meets mangroves and marine ecosystems along the Pacific coast. A dynamic zone where jungle meets ocean, supporting rich wildlife on land and offshore.

Conservation Area 08

Huetar Norte

Northern Rainforests, Rivers & Wetlands

Vast rainforests, rivers, and wetlands nourished by heavy rainfall. Lowland jungle supports complex ecosystems including tropical birds, amphibians, and expansive agricultural buffers.

Conservation Area 09

Tortuguero

Canals, Wetlands & Sea Turtle Nesting Beaches

A labyrinth of canals, swamps, and rainforest along the Caribbean coast. Globally known for sea turtle nesting beaches and intricate wetland ecosystems teeming with aquatic life.

Conservation Area 10

Tempisque

Wetlands, Mangroves & River Basin Ecosystems

Wetlands, mangroves, and dry tropical forest mosaics. Seasonal flooding and tidal influence shape critical habitat for migratory birds and aquatic species.

Conservation Area 11

Marina Cocos

Remote Oceanic Paradise & Marine Biodiversity Sanctuary

Far offshore, Cocos Island is a remote sanctuary of dense rainforest surrounded by rich marine biodiversity — famous for sharks, rays, and untouched tropical forest.
